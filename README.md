# Analysis-of-Purcari-wine-company
I conducted an analysis of Purcari, the Moldovan company covering 2022 to 2024.The company's revenue showed an upward trend,however operating expenses including cost of sales,marketing and administrative costs increased alongside revenue.As a result,net profit fell even though the sales went up.

Tools I have used:
1)Financial statements of company during 2022-2024
2)Excel-data collection and organization
3)PowerBI-data visualization

The data was collected from publicly available financial reports: https://purcariwineries.com/en/annual-reports/#
During this analysis I have put my focus on these key financial indicators:Revenue,Net profit,Net Profit Margin,Operating Expenses,Cost of Sales,Marketing and Sales Expenses,General and Administrative Expenses

My PowerBI dashboard includes:
1)KPI cards displaying key financial indicators
2)Revenue and Net Profit visualizations
3)Year selection filter (2022–2024)
4)Breakdown of major operating expenses
5)Comparative visualization of revenue, profit, and expenses

The aim of my analysis was to investigate the reason for the drop in net profit amid continued revenue growth.


My reccomendation for Purcari company is to monitor cost of sales,marketing, sales,general and administrative expenses
