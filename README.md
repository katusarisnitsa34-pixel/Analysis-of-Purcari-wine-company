# Analysis-of-Purcari-wine-company
I conducted an analysis of Purcari, the Moldovan company covering 2022 to 2024.The company's revenue showed an upward trend,however operating expenses including cost of sales,marketing and administrative costs increased alongside revenue.As a result,net profit fell even though the sales went up.

## Tools I have used:
1)Financial statements of company during 2022-2024
2)Excel-data collection and organization
3)PowerBI-data visualization

The data was collected from publicly available financial reports: https://purcariwineries.com/en/annual-reports/#
During this analysis I have put my focus on these key financial indicators:Revenue,Net profit,Net Profit Margin,Operating Expenses,Cost of Sales,Marketing and Sales Expenses,General and Administrative Expenses,ROA,ROE

## My PowerBI dashboard includes:
1)KPI cards displaying key financial indicators
2)Revenue and Net Profit visualizations
3)Year selection filter (2022–2024)
4)Breakdown of major operating expenses
5)Comparative visualization of revenue, profit, and expenses
6)Changing of ROA,ROE

The aim of my analysis was to investigate the reason for the drop in net profit amid continued revenue growth.

## Conclusion
During the data analysis, I found that the decline in net profit was accompanied by an increase in operating expenses outpacing revenue growth. Furthermore, I calculated ROA and ROE and also identified their downward trend. A drop in ROA indicates that the company’s assets (in this case, vineyards, equipment, and warehouses) are being used less efficiently, while a decline in ROE means that equity generates lower returns for shareholders.

## My recommendation
My recommendation for Purcari company is to monitor cost of sales,marketing, sales,general and administrative expenses,optimize supply chains and select only those sales channels that truly contribute to business development.
As for ROA, the company should increase its asset turnover and optimize its inventory.The company can boost it by selling off unused or low-efficiency assets, or increasing net profit , or do this simultaneously.
As for ROE, company can boost it by increasing net profit by raising prices on high-margin products, and removing unprofitable items from its product range,reduce the share of low-margin products,thereby increase the numerator of ROA (net profit).
